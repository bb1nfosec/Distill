## Approach that works

Do NOT send mass DMs. Instead:

1. **Reply to relevant threads** - when someone posts about Claude costs, token limits, or LLM tooling, reply with a specific insight from distill's benchmarks. Not a link drop - an actual insight, then a link.

2. **Write one good blog post** - "Your LLM costs are quadratic, not linear" with the chart from the web app. Post to dev.to and HN. This gets shared by amplifiers organically.

3. **@AnthropicAI and @OpenAI** - they actively retweet third-party tooling for their APIs. A clean tweet with the demo GIF and their @mention gets noticed.

4. **Timing for HN** - Tue–Thu 8–10am ET. Be in the comments immediately after posting. The first 30 minutes determine front page.
